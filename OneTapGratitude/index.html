One-Tap Gratitude

A tap. A thought. No journaling theatre.

One-Tap Gratitude makes it simple to capture a tiny moment each day. Open the app, pick a spark, write a line, and save. That's it. Over time the habit sticks, and your journal becomes a surprisingly uplifting record of the good in your life.

Download on the App Store

What it does

  • Fast. Type a sentence, tap save, done.
  • Helpful prompts. Stuck? Try "A person who helped me" or "A tiny moment I enjoyed."
  • Streaks that motivate. Consistency is marked with subtle animations and haptics, not nagging.
  • Private by design. Your entries stay on your device unless you choose to share them.
  • Export anytime. JSON and CSV for backups, plus a simple poster to share a favourite note.
  • Gentle reminders. Pick a time and get a friendly nudge, scheduled on your device.

Price

Free. There is no subscription and nothing to buy. The app is supported by advertising, and you can turn off ad personalisation from the first-launch prompt or in iOS Settings at any time without losing a single feature.

Privacy

There is no account and no server. Entries live in the app's own storage on your device, and deleting the app deletes them. The full details, including what Google receives to serve the ads, are in the privacy policy.
