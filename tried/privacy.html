Tried — Privacy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Tried is a first-foods list for two parents. There is no account.

What we collect

Food names, times, outcomes, notes, and the name you put on a row stay on your phones. Tried does not run a backend for the list. If you invite another phone on the same Apple Account, Apple iCloud / CloudKit carries the list between those phones. That traffic is Apple's, not ours.

Anonymous pings

The app may send a small ping when it launches, when you log the first food, when you send an invite, and when you see or buy Unlock. A ping contains the app name, an anonymous install id, the event name, the app version, and the OS version. It never contains a food name, a note, or your Apple ID.

Notifications

Reminders are optional, off by default, and scheduled on the phone. They do not go through our servers.

Purchases

Tried Unlock is sold through Apple. Apple handles the payment. We do not see your card.

Ads and tracking

None. Tried has no advertising SDK and does not track you across apps.

Deleting your data

Settings → Clear the list removes the rows on this phone. Deleting the app removes the local list. iCloud copies follow the devices signed in to that Apple Account.

Children

Tried is used by parents. It is not directed at children under 13 and knowingly collects no personal information from a child.
