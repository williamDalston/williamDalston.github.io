Tried — Support

Tried: first foods, both parents. Log what they just ate, and whether it was fine, a watch, or a reaction.

How to log

Tap Log a food. Type the food. Time is now unless you change it. Tap Fine, Watch, or Reaction, then Save. That is the whole job.

How to invite

Tap Invite, or Invite the other parent on the log sheet, and send Tried. On phones signed in to the same Apple Account, the list is the same list. Logging still works if the other parent has not installed yet.

If two phones disagree, the row's time and the name on the row are the source. Tried does not run a server that can break a tie.

Widget

Touch and hold the home screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Tried. The small widget shows the last food and who logged it.

Reminders

Off by default. Settings → Reminder after a first try. You pick the number of days. Missing a day does nothing.

Export and Unlock

JSON export is in Settings. The pediatrician PDF, history past two weeks, and a second child are Tried Unlock, $9.99 once. The log and the invite stay free.

Delete the list

Settings → Clear the list, or delete the app.
