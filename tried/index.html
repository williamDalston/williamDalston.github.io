The tap

Food. Now. Fine, watch, or reaction. Invite the other parent from that same screen.

The other phone

No account. On phones signed in to the same Apple Account, the list is the same list. The home-screen widget shows the last food and who logged it.

What it does not do

  • Tell you what is safe to give
  • Identify food from a photo
  • Track sleep, diapers, bottles, or pumping
  • Build a streak
  • Open an account
  • Sell a shop or a meal plan
