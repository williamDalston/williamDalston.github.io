timbre — Privacy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

timbre keeps the sound of the people and places you love. It is built so that nothing you record ever leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. timbre has no account, no sign-in, and no server. Your recordings, transcripts, colors, people, and places live only in the app's private storage on your device.

Where your recordings go

They stay on your phone. Transcription and sound understanding happen entirely on-device using Apple's frameworks. Audio and text are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. There is no "us" to send them to.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, your recordings are copied to your own private iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. This is Apple's iCloud, governed by Apple's privacy terms; the developer never has access to that data. Sync is off by default, and you can turn it off at any time.

Optional location

If you turn on location for places, timbre stores the coordinates of a moment on your device and looks up a place name using Apple's geocoder. Off unless you turn it on.

Analytics & ads

None. timbre contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking of any kind. No third-party code touches your voice.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete everything removes every recording and its data immediately and permanently. Deleting the app also removes everything (unless you enabled iCloud sync, in which case manage those copies in iOS Settings → iCloud).

Children

timbre is not directed at children under 13 and knowingly collects no personal information from anyone.
