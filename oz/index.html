Both phones need the last pump.

Both phones need the last pump. When it was, how many ounces, and which fridge the bag is in. One tap at the pump. The other phone updates.

The tap

Time is now. Side is left, right, or both. Ounces if you counted them. Save.

Ounces are optional on purpose. A session logged at three in the morning with nothing typed is still a session, and the tap always finishes.

The other phone

Invite the other parent from the same screen you log on. They open the link on their own phone, under their own Apple Account, and from then on both of you keep one log: the same sessions, the same stash, either of you logging. It runs on iCloud, so there is no account to make and no server of ours in between. The widget shows hours since the last pump and today's total without anyone opening anything.

Stash

Bags, with the amount, the date, and which fridge: home, work, freezer, or daycare. Oldest at the top, because that is the one that should leave next. Mark a bag used when it goes. There is no countdown to quitting and no days-of-milk-left number.

What it does not do

  • Tell you how much you should pump
  • Predict when you can stop
  • Track sleep, diapers, nursing, or growth
  • Identify a flange from a photo
  • Build a streak
  • Open an account
  • Sell a pump, a bag, or a shop

Oz does not say how much you should make. That is a lactation consultant. This only remembers what you already pumped.

Money

The log, the invite, and the last-pump widget are free, and stay free. Oz Year is $29.99 a year and adds a PDF, history past two weeks, and unlimited stash. Monthly and lifetime sit in the same purchase sheet.
