Oz — Privacy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Oz remembers when you pumped, how much, and which fridge the bag is in. There is no account and no Oz server holding any of it.

What we collect

None of your log. Sessions, ounces, minutes, bags, locations, and the name you put on the rows live in the app's storage on your phone.

Where the log lives

On your phone, and in your own private iCloud database if you are signed in to iCloud. That copy is between your phone and Apple, under your Apple Account. We cannot read it. If you are not signed in to iCloud, Oz works exactly the same and the log simply stays on the one phone.

What inviting the other parent shares

Inviting someone creates an iCloud share, the same mechanism as a shared album or a shared note. It is run by Apple between the two of you. We are not a participant and there is no Oz server in the middle.

Whoever accepts can see and change everything in the log: every session, every ounce, every bag and location, and the names on the rows. That is the point of it, and it is worth knowing before you send the link. The invitation is to a specific person, not a public link, and you can stop sharing at any time from the same screen you sent it from.

Anonymous counts

Oz sends a small anonymous event when the app opens and when a session is first logged, so we can tell whether anyone is using it. Each event carries the app's bundle id, a random identifier created on your phone, the event name, the app version, and the iOS version. That is the whole payload.

It never contains ounces, minutes, notes, bag locations, names, your email, your contacts, or any identifier Apple gives us. There is no advertising identifier, no tracking across apps, and no ATT prompt, because there is nothing to ask about.

Advertising

None. Oz contains no advertising and no third-party advertising code.

Notifications

The reminder is optional, off until you turn it on, and scheduled entirely on your phone at the interval you pick. Nothing is sent from a server, and no push token is collected.

Purchases

Oz Year, Oz Monthly, and Oz Lifetime are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether a purchase is currently active.

Deleting your data

Settings, then Clear everything, removes every session and bag from the phone and from your iCloud copy. Deleting the app removes the log from that phone as well.

Children

Oz is for the adults doing the pumping. It is not directed at children and knowingly collects no personal information from anyone.
