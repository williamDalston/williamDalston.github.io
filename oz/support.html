Oz — Support

Last pump, ounces, which fridge.

Log a session

Tap Pumped now. The time is already set to now, and the side is already set to both. Type the ounces if you counted them, or do not. Tap Save. A session with no amount is a complete session.

Change the time

Tap change next to Now, then pick the time. You cannot log a session in the future.

Add a stash bag

Open Stash from the toolbar, tap the plus, and enter the amount, the date it went in, and which fridge: home, work, freezer, or daycare. The oldest unused bag sits at the top, because that is the one that should leave next. Tap Used when a bag goes, and Undo if you tapped it by mistake.

Get the other parent on it

Tap the invite button in the toolbar, or the invite row on the log sheet, then send the link however you like. When they open it on their phone, Oz opens and both of you are on one log from then on. Either of you can log a session, and the rows say who did.

Both phones need to be signed in to iCloud. If this one is not, the invite screen says so, and the log on this phone keeps working regardless.

To see who has joined, or to stop sharing, open the same invite screen again.

If two phones disagree

The row's own timestamp and the name on it are the record. There is no server of ours deciding which phone is right.

Add the widget

Touch and hold the home screen until the apps wiggle, tap Edit, then Add Widget, then search for Oz. The small widget shows hours since the last pump and today's total. Tapping it opens the log sheet.

The reminder

Settings, then Remind me. Pick the interval, from one to twelve hours. It counts from your last session and fires on the phone. It is not a streak, and skipping it costs you nothing.

Ounces or millilitres

Settings, then Units. Old sessions keep the unit you logged them in and are converted for display, so switching back and forth never rewrites anything.

Export

Settings, then Export as JSON, which is free and includes everything. Export as PDF gives you sessions and stash on paper and is part of Oz Year.

Oz Year

$29.99 a year, with monthly and lifetime in the same sheet. It adds the PDF, history past two weeks, and unlimited stash. The log, the invite, and the last-pump widget are free and stay free. Purchases restore from Settings.

Delete the log

Settings, then Clear everything, removes every session and bag from the phone and from your iCloud copy. Deleting the app clears that phone too.
